The idea

Most phone cameras quietly merge several frames and “fix” your shot before you ever see it. Lazy Cam does the opposite — one frame, then a film look you actually control.

Pick a look and shoot. When you want more, bend the tone curve, shift colors by hue, add grain, or go black and white. What you see in the viewfinder is what lands in your library — no surprises after the shutter.

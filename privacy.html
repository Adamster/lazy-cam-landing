Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Lazy Cam does not collect, store, or share your personal data.

No accounts · No tracking · No analytics

Lazy Cam is a camera app that runs entirely on your device. We built it so your photos and your settings stay yours. Here is exactly what that means.

Your photos

Photos you take are saved straight to your device’s Photo Library. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Camera & Photos access

The app asks for camera access so you can take photos, and Photo Library access so it can save them. Those permissions are used for nothing else.

Presets & settings

Your custom presets and app settings are stored on your device. If you keep iCloud sync turned on, they’re also stored in your own private iCloud account so they sync across your devices. We have no access to this data, and you can turn iCloud sync off at any time in the app’s Settings.

Diagnostics

The app may keep on-device performance diagnostics to help it run smoothly. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us.

No analytics or tracking

Lazy Cam contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking technology of any kind.

Children’s privacy

Lazy Cam does not collect data from anyone, including children, and contains no accounts or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. Because we don’t collect personal data, we have nothing to sell or share, and there is no account to delete.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at support@notlazy.org and we’ll be glad to help.
